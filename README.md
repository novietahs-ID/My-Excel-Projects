📊 Excel Data Analysis Project
🚀 Project Overview
This project demonstrates the use of Excel to transform raw business data into actionable insights through data analysis, reporting, and visualisation. The analysis focused on sales performance, customer trends, and operational metrics to support data-driven decision-making and risk-aware business planning.
🔍 Key Analytics Capabilities
📈 Data Analysis & Insights
•	Analysed sales and customer data to identify trends, patterns, and performance drivers
•	Delivered business insights to support strategic and operational decision-making
•	Applied data exploration techniques to improve reporting accuracy and transparency
•	Generated evidence-based recommendations from complex datasets
📊 Reporting & Visualisation
•	Developed interactive dashboards and visual reports
•	Monitored performance trends across products, regions, and customer segments
•	Presented insights in a clear, stakeholder-friendly format
•	Enhanced data storytelling through charts and summary reports
⚠️ Risk & Performance Insights
•	Identified data patterns that may indicate business risks or opportunities
•	Evaluated performance against key metrics and objectives
•	Supported risk-informed decision-making through data validation and trend analysis
•	Improved visibility of business performance through structured reporting
🛠️ Tools & Technologies
Microsoft Excel | PivotTables | Pivot Charts | Data Modelling | Data Visualisation | Business Reporting
🎯 Key Outcomes
•	Transformed raw data into actionable business intelligence
•	Strengthened analytical thinking and problem-solving capabilities
•	Improved reporting efficiency and insight generation
•	Developed practical experience in data-driven decision support
